How to claim

  1. Install the UniSat wallet from the official site and switch to the network shown above.
  2. Press Connect UniSat.
  3. Pick a lot and press Claim. Check the amount, price and fee.
  4. Approve once in UniSat. Payment and tokens move in one transaction.
You pay and receive a BRC-20 transfer inscription in one transaction, signed in your own wallet. Nobody ever needs your seed phrase or private key. This page never asks for one. You need two separate coins in your wallet (press "Prepare wallet" if you have one).
